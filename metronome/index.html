How to use the Metronome

  1. Set a comfortable speed. BPM means beats per minute: a lower number is slower. Try 60 to begin.
  2. Press Start and play along with the steady click. Press Stop when you finish.
  3. Leave Beats at 4 to count along: 1, 2, 3, 4. Subdivision adds extra clicks between beats; leave it at 1 to start.
  4. Tap a beat circle to make that click louder, normal or silent.

Ready to build speed? Stop playback and turn on Speed trainer. Set your starting and target speeds, how much to increase and how many times to repeat before speeding up. One repetition is a full round of the beat circles.
