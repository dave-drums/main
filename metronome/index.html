How to Use the Online Metronome

  1. Set your tempo in beats per minute (BPM), or use Tap tempo to find a pulse.
  2. Choose Beats per Bar and a subdivision to set the pulse and hear smaller rhythmic divisions within each beat.
  3. Click a beat circle to cycle between its accent, normal and muted states.
  4. Press Start to practice with the click, then Stop when you finish.

Use Tempo trainer to increase or decrease the BPM by a chosen amount every chosen number of bars, up to the Stop at tempo. Opening the trainer stops playback; choose your settings, close it and press Start when you are ready.

Use Full screen when supported by your browser. Select Exit full screen or press Escape to return to the page.

Start at a tempo where you can play comfortably and consistently. Listen to how your notes line up with the click, then increase the BPM gradually as your timing settles.

This free online BPM metronome with subdivisions works in your browser with JavaScript and an internet connection. To write and hear your own drum patterns, try Groove Lab's free drum groove maker. For individual guidance, explore private drum lessons in Campbellfield, Melbourne.
