01 / THE APPROACH

Lessons Built Around You

Every student brings different experience, musical interests and goals. My private drum lessons are personalised to help you develop strong foundations, confidence and your own voice behind the kit.

Technique, coordination and rhythmic understanding connect directly to playing music. Alongside timing and groove, we explore creativity and expression, helping you become a confident, versatile musician over time.

What You Can Learn

  • Technique & Coordination

  • Rhythm, Timing & Groove

  • Songs & Musicality

  • Creativity & Expression

  • Advanced Concepts & Theory

  • Personalised Learning & Development

02 / YOUR STARTING POINT

For Every Stage of Your Playing

Your experience, interests and progress shape each lesson. These are starting points for an approach tailored to you.

Beginners

Learn the fundamentals, develop coordination, understand rhythm and start playing your favourite songs.

Intermediate Drummers

Improve technique, timing, independence, fills, musical vocabulary and overall confidence behind the kit.

Advanced Drummers

Explore complex rhythms, odd metres, polyrhythms, metric modulation, advanced coordination and musical expression.

03 / YOUR TEACHER

Learn with a Working Drummer

I'm Dave D'Amore, a professional drummer with around 30 years of playing experience across live performance, recording and session work. My musical background spans progressive rock, metal, funk, fusion and groove-based music.

My teaching experience includes seven years running my own drum school in Cairns, where I taught more than 50 students. I now offer private lessons at my studio in Campbellfield, Melbourne.

Students benefit from the practical perspective of a working musician, connecting technical development with musical feel, creativity and playing real songs. Read more about my drumming background.

04 / THE DETAILS

Drum Lessons in Campbellfield & Melbourne's Northern Suburbs

All lessons take place at my Campbellfield studio. Students from Broadmeadows, Fawkner, Glenroy, Hadfield, Pascoe Vale, Coolaroo, Meadow Heights and Dallas can travel here for private tuition.

Students from Thomastown, Lalor, Reservoir, Coburg, Preston, Epping and Craigieburn are also welcome. Enquire about lessons in Campbellfield to discuss your playing goals and availability.

Between lessons, work on timing with the free online metronome or write and play practice patterns in Groove Lab's drum notation editor.
