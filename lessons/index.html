THE APPROACH

Lessons built around you

We start with where you are and what you want to play. Each lesson balances focused work on your technique with putting it into practice through grooves, songs and creative ideas.

There's room to slow things down, ask questions and work through a challenge. Your interests and progress guide what we work on next.

What you can learn

  • Technique & coordination

    Develop control, coordination and efficient movement.

  • Rhythm, timing & groove

    Build a steadier pulse and a stronger feel.

  • Songs & musicality

    Put technique to work in the music you enjoy.

  • Creativity & expression

    Develop ideas, vocabulary and your own voice.

  • Advanced concepts & theory

    Explore odd metres, polyrhythms and metric modulation.

  • Personalised learning & development

    Work at a pace shaped around your interests and progress.

YOUR STARTING POINT

For every stage of your playing

Your experience, interests and progress shape each lesson. These are starting points for an approach tailored to you.

Beginners

Learn the fundamentals, develop coordination, understand rhythm and start playing your favourite songs.

Intermediate drummers

Improve technique, timing, independence, fills, musical vocabulary and overall confidence behind the kit.

Advanced drummers

Explore complex rhythms, odd metres, polyrhythms, metric modulation, advanced coordination and musical expression.

YOUR TEACHER

Learn with a working drummer

I'm Dave D'Amore, a professional drummer with 30 years of playing experience across live performance, recording and session work. My musical background spans progressive rock, metal, funk, fusion and groove-based music.

I spent seven years running my own drum school in Cairns, teaching more than 50 students. I've also taught in schools, and now offer private lessons at my studio in Campbellfield, Melbourne.

Students benefit from the practical perspective of a working musician, connecting technical development with musical feel, creativity and playing real songs. Read more about my drumming background.

Drum lesson FAQs

Do you teach complete beginners?

Yes. Lessons accommodate complete beginners through to experienced drummers.

What ages do you teach?

Lessons are available for ages 10 and above.

Where are lessons held?

Private lessons are held at my studio in Campbellfield, Melbourne's northern suburbs.

Do you teach advanced drummers?

Yes. Lessons can focus on advanced technique, coordination, rhythmic concepts and musical development.

What styles can I learn?

Lessons can explore a wide range of styles, including rock, metal, funk, fusion and progressive music, depending on the student's interests.
