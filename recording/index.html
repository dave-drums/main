Behind the Kit

I'm Dave D'Amore, a Melbourne-based professional drummer with around 30 years of playing experience. My background spans progressive rock, metal, fusion and groove-based music. I bring that experience to recording acoustic drum performances shaped around your music, with feel, dynamics and creativity serving the song.

I also play guitar and bass, and have experience in songwriting, arrangements, recording, production and mixing. That broader perspective helps me understand how drum parts interact with other instruments and develop performances that complement the whole arrangement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does drum recording cost?+

Pricing depends on the complexity of the music, number of tracks and recording requirements. Get in touch with some details about your project for a personalised quote.

Can you record drums for artists outside Australia?+

Yes. Remote drum recording is available to artists, bands and producers worldwide.

Can you work from existing demos or programmed drums?+

Existing demos and programmed drum arrangements can be used as references when discussing a recording project.

Can you tailor the drum performance to my music?+

Absolutely. Each performance is developed around your music, including the arrangement, groove, dynamics and playing style.

What recording files will I receive?+

Recording deliverables can include individual raw mono drum tracks or five mixed stereo drum stems covering kick, snare, toms, overheads and room.

How long does it take to receive my recordings?+

Turnaround times depend on the project and my recording schedule. I'll confirm an estimated delivery timeframe before we get started.

Can I request revisions to the drum performance?+

Yes. Each song includes two rounds of revisions for reasonable adjustments to drum parts, fills, grooves and other performance details. Additional revisions are $50 AUD per round. Major changes to the song's structure, tempo or arrangement after recording may incur additional charges.

How do I get started?+

Get in touch with details about your project, including your music, references and recording requirements.
