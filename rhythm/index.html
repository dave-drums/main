How to use Rhythm Reader

  1. Generate & loop: choose a meter, difficulty, divisions per beat and amount of space. New rhythm creates another exercise; Start loops it.
  2. Continuous reading: follow the current bar with the next bar shown ahead. New bars keep arriving until you stop.
  3. Play along: tap the pad, press J, or use Space outside a control during playback. Your timing, accuracy and streak update as you play. Stop shows the result; Drill missed bars repeats the bars that need work.
  4. Practice options include swing, sounds, click subdivisions, individual beat accents/mutes, silent bars, a speed trainer and a session timer. BPM follows the displayed beat unit: dotted quarters in compound meters and eighth notes in 7/8.

Rests are silent. A dot adds half a note’s value; a curved tie continues a note without another attack. Tuplet numbers change the spacing. Swing shifts the halfway point of each beat and is disabled for 3 or 6 divisions.

For tap-along, use Adjust timing and tap eight steady clicks. Scores are practice feedback, influenced by your input and audio setup; recalibrate after changing devices. Saved exercises stay in this browser. Share links reproduce generated exercises.

Start slowly and keep the rhythm relaxed. Space starts/stops outside controls in the other modes. Leaving the tab pauses audio; Escape exits fullscreen.
