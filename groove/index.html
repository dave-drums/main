How to use Groove Lab

  1. The grid reads from left to right. Each row represents a drum or cymbal. Click or tap a cell to add a hit; tap it again to remove it.
  2. Press the play triangle to hear your pattern, and the stop square to stop. Adjust Tempo to change the speed. BPM means beats per minute.
  3. The note setting controls the grid spacing. Start with 1/8 NOTES; choose 1/16 NOTES for more positions between beats.
  4. Use Share to copy a link that saves your pattern. Keep the link to reopen it later, or send it to someone else. Print and Download provide other export options.

More note options: Right-click a note, or turn on Advanced edit to open its options with a tap. You can add accents (stronger hits), ghost notes (quieter hits) and other variations.

On a phone, landscape orientation gives the grid more room.
