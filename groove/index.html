GROOVE LAB

A free online drum groove maker and notation editor. Create, play and share your own drum patterns.

PLEASE ROTATE YOUR DEVICE FOR BEST VIEWING

Create a Drum Groove with Groove Lab

Write drum notation online, hear how your pattern sounds and use it in your practice.

  1. Choose a time signature and subdivision, then select notes in the drum grid to build your beat.
  2. Adjust the tempo and press Play to listen while following the notation.
  3. Use Share to copy a link to your groove. Print the notation or download your work using the editor's controls.

On a phone, rotate to landscape for more editing space or pan within the editor. Use Full screen when available. The editor needs JavaScript and an internet connection. If it does not appear, open the Groove Lab editor directly.

For focused timing practice, try the free online metronome with subdivisions. For help developing your grooves, explore private drum lessons in Campbellfield, Melbourne.
