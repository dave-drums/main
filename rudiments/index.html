How to use the Rudiment Trainer

  1. Choose a rudiment: a short pattern of drum strokes. Single Stroke Roll is a good starting point. Choose Right hand or Left hand to set the lead for the pattern.
  2. Set Tempo to a comfortable speed. BPM means beats per minute; a lower number is slower.
  3. Press Start and follow the highlighted notes. The letters below show which hand to use: blue R for right, amber L for left. Press the stop square to finish.
  4. Metronome controls the steady click; Drums controls the pattern sound. Mute Drums to practice the pattern yourself with just the click.

Reading the notes: An accent (>) means play that stroke stronger than the surrounding notes. Small notes are grace notes: light strokes played just before the main stroke.

Speed trainer: Press Speed trainer to open its settings, then enable it. Set your starting and target BPM, the increase at each step and the repetitions before each increase. One repetition is the complete displayed pattern. Stop playback to edit these settings.
