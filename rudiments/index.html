How to use the Rudiment Trainer

  1. Choose a pattern. Single Stroke Roll is a good place to start.
  2. Choose which hand starts. Under the notes, R means right hand and L means left hand.
  3. Set a comfortable speed. BPM means beats per minute: a lower number is slower.
  4. Press Start and play along with the highlighted notes. Use Metronome to turn the steady click on or off.

Reading the notes: > means play louder. Small notes are light taps just before the main note.

Ready to build speed? Stop playback and turn on Speed trainer. Set your starting and target speeds, how much to increase and how many times to repeat each pattern before speeding up.
